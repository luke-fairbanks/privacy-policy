# Lectern support

Lectern reads your documents aloud with natural voices that run entirely on your
iPhone, made by Faba Development / Luke Fairbanks.

For anything not answered below, email
[luke_fairbanks@icloud.com](mailto:luke_fairbanks@icloud.com) with "Lectern" in
the subject. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you are on, and what you
were doing when the problem happened. Do not send passwords or payment details.

## Common questions

### Which files can I import?

PDF (including scanned PDFs), EPUB, Word (.docx), plain text, Markdown, RTF,
photos of pages, and pasted text. Use the + button in the library, the share
sheet from Files or Safari, or Scan Pages for printed text.

### Does it need an internet connection?

No. The voices are built into the app. Only purchases use the network.

### Playback stopped when I locked my phone

It should not. Lectern keeps playing in the background and shows controls on the
lock screen. If it stops, check that Lectern has not been force-quit from the app
switcher, that Low Power Mode is not restricting background activity, and that
another app has not taken over audio.

### Where did my place go?

Lectern saves your position every few seconds and whenever you pause. The library
shows how far you are in every document, and "Continue listening" picks up
where you left off, to the sentence.

### A voice is marked PRO

Two studio voices are free. The rest, along with whole books past the free
preview, an unlimited library, and audio export, come with Lectern Pro. Tap the
voice to hear a preview before deciding.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

In iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Lectern Pro is billed by Apple, so
we cannot cancel it for you. The Lifetime option is a one-time purchase with
nothing to cancel.

### How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Open Lectern, go to Settings › Restore Purchases, signed into the same Apple
Account you bought with.

### A document imported with no text

Some PDFs are pictures of pages rather than text. Lectern recognizes text in
scanned PDFs on the device, but very low-resolution scans or handwriting may
not be readable. Try Scan Pages with the camera for a printed original.
