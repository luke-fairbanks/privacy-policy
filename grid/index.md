# Grid — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 10 September 2026**

Grid is a feed planner. It runs on your device and it is built so that there is
very little about you for anyone to collect.

## Your photos never leave your device

When you add a photo to a feed, Grid copies it into its own private storage on
your iPhone and makes a small thumbnail to draw the grid with. That copy stays
on the device. It is not uploaded, not backed up to any server we run, and not
shared with anyone.

Grid uses the system photo picker, which means it never asks for access to your
photo library. You hand Grid the specific images you choose, and it can see
nothing else.

Re-encoding a photo on import also strips its EXIF metadata, so any location
recorded by your camera is discarded rather than stored.

The one photo permission Grid ever asks for is permission to **add** images to
your library, and only at the moment you tap Save to export split tiles or a
preview.

## There is no account

Grid has no sign-in, no username, no email address and no profile. It never
connects to Instagram, Meta, or any other social network, and it cannot post
anything on your behalf.

## Captions, notes and dates

Anything you type into Grid — a caption draft, a private note, a planned date —
is stored on your device with the rest of the plan and is never transmitted.

## Purchases

If you subscribe to Grid Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple. Grid uses
**RevenueCat** to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives
an anonymous identifier it generates for the installation, and the purchase
history attached to it. It does not receive your name, your email address, your
photos, or anything you have typed into the app. Grid never calls `logIn`, so
that identifier is not linked to any identity.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>

## Analytics

The released build of Grid ships without an analytics key and therefore
transmits no usage data at all. If that ever changes, this notice will be
updated before the build that changes it is released.

## Children

Grid is rated 4+ and collects nothing, so there is nothing about a child to
collect.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a post removes its stored copy. Deleting a feed removes every photo in
it. Deleting Grid from your iPhone removes everything at once — there is no
copy anywhere else to ask us to delete.
