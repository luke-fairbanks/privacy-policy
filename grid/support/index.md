# Grid — Support

## How Grid works

Grid shows you what your profile grid will look like before you publish
anything. It never posts for you, and it never connects to any social account.

### Planned posts sit above published ones

A new post lands at the top left of a profile and pushes everything else down,
so anything you are planning appears above what you have already published.

Two things follow from that, and both are deliberate:

- **You cannot reorder published posts.** Neither can you on a real profile, so
  letting the grid pretend otherwise would make your plan wrong.
- **The next post to publish is the planned tile closest to your published
  ones** — not the first one. The last thing you post ends up highest, so the
  queue flows upward into your feed. Grid marks that tile **NEXT**.

### Rearranging

Touch and hold a planned photo, then drag. A quick swipe scrolls the grid
instead. Published photos do not move.

### Hiding a post

Open any post and choose *Hide from preview* to see what your profile would
look like without it. Nothing is deleted, and *Show in preview* brings it back.

### Splitting a photo

Choose a photo, pick 3x1, 3x2 or 3x3, and Grid cuts it into tiles. **Post them
in the order Grid numbers them — tile 1 first.** Because the newest post goes to
the top left, the last tile you publish is the one that ends up there. Posting
them in reading order puts the picture in upside down.

## Common questions

**Does Grid post to my account?**
No. Grid has no connection to any social network and no way to publish anything.

**Do my photos get uploaded?**
No. They are copied into Grid's own storage on your iPhone and stay there.

**Why does Grid not ask for photo library access?**
It uses the system picker, so you hand over only the photos you select. Grid
never sees the rest of your library.

**I bought Pro and it is not showing.**
Open the paywall and tap **Restore purchases**. Make sure you are signed in with
the Apple Account that made the purchase.

**How do I cancel?**
Settings → Manage subscription in Grid, or iPhone Settings → your name →
Subscriptions.

**My photos disappeared.**
This should not happen — Grid stores photos by name and resolves them fresh
each launch, precisely so an iOS update cannot break them. If it does, please
email us with your iOS version and what you did last.
