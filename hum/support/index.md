# Hum support

Hum is a tone, noise and binaural-beat generator for iPhone, made by Faba
Development / Luke Fairbanks.

For anything not answered below, email
[luke_fairbanks@icloud.com](mailto:luke_fairbanks@icloud.com) with "Hum" in the
subject. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you are on, and what you were
doing when the problem happened. Do not send passwords or payment details.

## Common questions

### I cannot hear the binaural beat

Binaural beats need headphones. Each ear is sent a slightly different
frequency, and the effect depends on those two tones staying separate — over a
speaker they mix in the air before they reach you, and the effect does not
occur. This is how binaural beats work generally, not a limitation of the app.

### The sound stops when I lock my phone

It should not — Hum keeps playing in the background and shows controls on the
lock screen. If it stops, check that Hum has not been force-quit from the app
switcher, and that a Focus or Screen Time rule is not suspending it.

### A high frequency sounds silent

Above roughly 15 kHz, whether you hear a tone depends on your hearing and your
speaker. Most phone speakers roll off well before 20 kHz, and adult hearing
usually does too. The tone is still being generated.

### I bought Pro but the app still shows Free

Open Settings in the app and tap **Restore purchases**. That asks the App Store
for anything previously bought with your Apple Account. If it still shows Free,
check you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.

### How do I cancel or change my subscription

Open Settings in the app and tap **Manage subscription**, which opens the App
Store's own subscription screen. You can also reach it in iOS Settings by
tapping your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal;
access continues until the current period ends.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

### I was not offered the free trial

Apple allows one introductory offer per subscription group per Apple Account,
ever. If you have subscribed to Hum before, the trial has already been used and
the purchase starts as a paid period. Hum only advertises the trial to accounts
the App Store reports as still eligible.

### What is free, and what needs Pro

Free covers the whole generator from 20 Hz to 20 kHz, all four waveforms, all
three noise colours, binaural beats, sleep timers, background playback, and 13
of the 17 presets. It also includes one saved mix and up to two layers in a
mix.

Pro adds unlimited layers, unlimited saved mixes, and the four layered presets.

### Do the frequency names mean anything

Names like "Solfeggio", "432 Hz" and "528 Hz" are the conventional names people
use for those numbers. They are labels, not claims. Hum makes no health or
medical claims, is not a medical device, and is not intended to diagnose,
treat, cure or prevent anything.

### Does Hum use my microphone

No. Every sound is generated on the device from numbers, and Hum's audio
session is set to playback only, so it never asks for microphone access.

Apple requires any app whose code references the microphone APIs to state a
reason for it, even when the app does not use them. Hum is built on a
general-purpose audio library that can record, which is why you may see a
microphone entry listed. Hum does not record, and you should never see a
permission prompt.

## Hearing safety

Listen at a comfortable level. Prolonged exposure to loud sound can damage
hearing, and sustained pure tones are harder to judge by ear than music is —
they can be louder than they seem. Hum starts at half volume for that reason.
If you have ringing or discomfort after listening, turn it down.
