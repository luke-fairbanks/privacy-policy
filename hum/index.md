# Hum privacy policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Hum is an offline tone, noise, and binaural-beat generator operated by Faba
Development / Luke Fairbanks ("we," "us"). This notice describes the app's
current data handling.

## Accounts and developer collection

Hum does not require an account and has no sign-in. The app code does not send
your saved mixes, favorite frequencies, settings, or any app-generated
identifier to developer-controlled servers. It does not use cloud sync,
advertising, or cross-app tracking.

The released build ships without an analytics key configured, so the bundled
analytics SDK is inert and no usage events leave your device. If a future
release turns analytics on, this notice will be updated first to describe what
is sent.

Apple, your device manufacturer, and the app store may independently process
app downloads, purchases, backups, and diagnostics under their own terms. We
may receive aggregated App Store analytics or diagnostics that you choose to
share with the platform.

## The microphone

Hum does not record. The app synthesizes every sound it plays — tones, noise,
and binaural beats are generated on the device from numbers, not captured from
your surroundings. It does not request microphone access and contains no
recording code.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in its local application storage:

- saved mixes: the name you give them, their layers and settings, and a local
  record identifier and creation timestamp;
- favorite frequencies;
- output volume and whether the one-time hearing-safety notice has been shown;
- whether onboarding has been completed; and
- a local session count and install date used to decide when to show Apple's
  rating prompt.

This information is protected by the operating system's app sandbox. Hum does
not add separate application-level encryption to its local storage. Device
backups may retain app data according to your operating-system and backup
settings.

## Audio playback and the lock screen

Hum continues playing while the app is in the background or the screen is
locked, and registers standard playback controls. The name of what is playing
appears on the lock screen, in the notification area, and in the Dynamic
Island, so it is visible to anyone who can see your screen. This information
stays on the device.

## Purchases

Hum Pro is sold through the App Store. Two parties are involved:

- **Apple** processes the purchase and manages the subscription. Hum does not
  receive your payment-card details.
- **RevenueCat** receives an app-specific user identifier, the product and
  entitlement, transaction and subscription-event information, and coarse
  device and country information, and uses it to provide and restore Pro
  access.

Hum does not create a RevenueCat identifier tied to your name or email address;
the app uses an anonymous identifier generated on the device.

If you restore purchases, the same information is used to match your Apple
account's prior transactions to your Pro entitlement.

## Rating prompt

The app may ask Apple to show its standard rating prompt after a few sessions.
Apple controls whether the prompt appears and what you submit. We do not
receive the rating or any review text through the app.

## Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete individual mixes, uninstall the app, or the
operating system clears the app's storage. Device backups may retain copies
separately.

Purchase and entitlement records held by Apple and RevenueCat are retained
under their own terms, because they are what allow a purchase to be restored on
a new device. To request deletion of the RevenueCat record associated with your
device, email us with "Hum privacy request."

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel it through
the App Store's subscription-management screen.

## Health and medical claims

Hum is a sound generator, not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose,
treat, cure, or prevent any condition, and we make no claims about health
effects of any particular frequency.

## Children

Hum is available to a general audience and does not transmit personal
information or user-created content to us. A parent or guardian with questions
may contact us using the address below.
