# Mane support


Mane previews a hairstyle or hair color on your own photo. Add a selfie, choose a style or color, and compare before and after.

## Getting a good preview

- Face the camera, head and shoulders in frame.
- Even light, ideally daylight from a window. Avoid a bright light directly behind you.
- Hair visible: no hats, hoods, headphones or sunglasses.
- Sharp and at normal zoom. Screenshots and heavy crops come out soft.
- One person per photo.

Previews are AI-generated approximations. They are a way to see whether a style suits you, not a guarantee of how a real cut or color will look.

## Common questions

**A preview failed or took too long.** Check your connection and try again. Previews usually take 10 to 20 seconds. If a photo is repeatedly declined, try a clearer one of a single person facing the camera.

**"You have used your free previews."** The first previews are free. Mane Pro adds unlimited previews, every hairstyle and color, custom descriptions and reference photos.

**Restoring a purchase.** Settings → Restore purchases, or the Restore link on the Pro screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

**Cancelling.** Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or Manage subscription inside Mane. Cancelling stops renewal; access continues to the end of the paid period.

**Deleting my photos.** Every look is stored only on your device. Delete one from History, or all of them in Settings → Delete all looks. Our servers keep no copy of your photos.

**Where do my photos go?** To create a preview, your photo is sent to Google's Gemini image model and the result is returned. Details are in the [privacy notice](../).
