# WriteOff support

WriteOff: Deduction Scanner helps U.S. self-employed people find out whether
an expense may be a write-off, see the IRS guidance it rests on, and keep the
records for tax time. Made by Faba Development LLC.

For anything not answered below, email
[luke_fairbanks@icloud.com](mailto:luke_fairbanks@icloud.com) with "WriteOff"
in the subject. Say which iPhone and iOS version you are on and what you were
doing. Do not send passwords, payment details or tax identification numbers.

## Common questions

### Is this tax advice?

No. WriteOff shows how published IRS guidance may apply to the facts you
enter and cites the sources. It cannot see your whole tax situation, and it
is not a substitute for a CPA, enrolled agent or tax attorney. Confirm
anything you plan to rely on with a professional.

### Why did it say "need more information" or "professional review"?

Because the facts given do not settle the question, or the situation is one
the app does not assess (for example partnerships, S corporations, employee
compensation, life insurance). That is deliberate: WriteOff says so rather
than guessing.

### The item was identified wrongly

Tap the item name to correct it, choose a different category, or pick one of
the alternatives shown. The rules use what you confirm, not the guess.

### Which tax years are supported?

2025 and 2026. Rules and dollar limits are applied only for the year you
choose. Earlier years are not assessed.

### Are state taxes covered?

No. WriteOff covers federal income tax only. State treatment can differ.

### Where are my records stored?

On your phone, in the app's private storage, protected by your passcode and
included in your device backups. There are no accounts and no cloud copy. If
you get a new phone, restore it from a backup to keep your records.

### What is sent to the AI provider?

Photos you scan (re-encoded to remove camera metadata), typed questions, and
the text recognized from receipts (never the receipt image) go to Google's
Gemini API. We store none of it, and Google does not train on content sent
through the paid API. Whether something is
deductible is decided by a rules engine from IRS sources, not by the AI. See
the Privacy Policy.

### How do I export for my accountant?

Records › Export. Choose CSV, a PDF summary, or a ZIP bundle with both and
every attached receipt. Export is part of WriteOff Pro.

### How do I cancel or restore a subscription?

Cancel in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Restore in WriteOff's
Settings › Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that made the
purchase.

### How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records from the record screen, or use Settings › "Delete
all records and profile". Deleting the app removes everything.
